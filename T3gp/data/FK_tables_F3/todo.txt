Steps to produce the full FK tables etc for the F3
For the CC just replace NC with CC.

python3 scripts/build_observable_cards.py
python3 scripts/build_pineko_yamldb.py
python3 scripts/generate_grids.py
pineko theory opcards 208 HERA_NC_318GEV_EM-SIGMARED
pineko theory ekos 208 HERA_NC_318GEV_EM-SIGMARED
pineko theory fks 208 HERA_NC_318GEV_EM-SIGMARED

the pineko commads are also all in build_fks.sh
scripts/ also contain compare_cc_fk_tables.py and analyze_one_fk.py to help with debugging and check if everything was generated correctly.
